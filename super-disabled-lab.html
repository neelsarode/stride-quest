SUPER ATTACK — disabled-state concepts

The current disabled state is 40% transparency — un-pixel-like. Four material-language alternatives below. Top plate = disabled while GOLD (walked to 0 energy, goal not hit); bottom plate = disabled while PURPLE (spent right after arming Overdrive). Tap a plate to toggle disabled ↔ enabled.

S — STONE SLEEP

The classic: a spent button is carved from slate. The whole plate swaps material; the meter and text stay legible but cold. Overdrive status survives as the white text row.

+ the most game-native read — every pixel-RPG player knows grey = not now.

− while Overdrive is armed the purple identity hides until you re-earn energy.

N — NIGHT RAMP

Same button, lights off: the gold (or amethyst) ramp is re-baked ~45% darker. Keeps each state's identity — a dark gold button and a dark purple button — just dormant.

+ keeps the gold/purple identity readable while clearly asleep; feels like the fuel "Resting" tone.

D — DITHER VEIL

A 50% pixel-checkerboard shadow over the normal plate — the retro "ghosted" treatment (Game Boy menus). Composes over ANY material unchanged.

+ maximum retro authenticity; one overlay works on gold, purple, and anything future.

− at 2x phone scale the checker is fine-grained — squint and it reads like… transparency.

K — EMPTY SOCKET

The plate becomes a dark recessed well with only the material's rim left glowing — an empty power socket waiting to be refilled. The word dims to stone; the gem shows 0.

+ the strongest "drained tank, walk to refill" story; the rim keeps the gold/purple identity.

All four keep the OVERDRIVE meter visible (your step progress isn't lost when energy hits 0 — walking is still doing something). Pick a letter — S / N / K need one extra bake each through the normal pipeline; D bakes a tiny repeating dither tile. We can also swap the "◆ 0" for a "WALK TO RECHARGE" hint on whichever wins.
